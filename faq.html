Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about HNAI.Researcher courses — completely honest answers from Humera Noor herself.

About the Courses
Are these courses really free? +
Yes — 100% free, forever. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no credit card required. All courses on HNAI.Researcher are completely open source and free for everyone. This is my personal commitment as an educator.
How are the courses taught? +
Each lesson is a carefully selected YouTube video from the world's best AI/ML educators. Every video is curated for quality, clarity and relevance. All practical work can be done in Google Colab — no installation needed.
Do I get a certificate when I complete a course? +
We are continuously improving HNAI.Researcher to give students the best learning experience. Stay tuned for exciting updates coming to the platform soon!
Who are these courses for? +
These courses are designed for anyone — from complete beginners with no programming experience to working professionals who want to upskill in AI and ML. Whether you are a student in Pakistan, an international learner, or an experienced developer, there is something here for you.
What is the AI Engineer Roadmap / Masterclass? +
The AI Engineer Roadmap is our flagship course — a single structured curriculum covering Python for AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and NLP & LLMs in 62 lessons. It is the complete journey from beginner to AI engineer, all in one place. Completely free.
Getting Started
Where do I start if I am a complete beginner? +
Start with Python for AI — it covers everything from the basics of Python to NumPy, Pandas, and data visualization. No prior experience needed. Then move to Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and NLP & LLMs in that order.
Do I need to install anything? +
No installation needed at all! All practical coding can be done in Google Colab — a free, browser-based Python environment provided by Google. Just open Colab, write code, and run it. No setup, no configuration, no headaches.
How much time does each course take? +
It depends on your pace. Most lessons are 20–40 minutes each. The full AI Engineer Roadmap (62 lessons) takes approximately 3–6 months if you study consistently. Individual courses range from 2 weeks to 2 months depending on depth and practice time.
Technical Questions
Videos are not playing — what should I do? +
Try these steps: (1) Tap the lesson name in the sidebar — the video should load in the player. (2) If on mobile, tap the menu button (☰) to open the sidebar. (3) Try refreshing the page. (4) If a specific video shows "unavailable", please contact us and we will replace it immediately.
Does my progress get saved? +
Yes! Your progress is automatically saved in your browser. When you return to a course, it will resume from where you left off. Note: progress is saved per browser/device, so it will not sync across different devices.
Is this website open source? +
Yes! The entire website is open source and available on GitHub at github.com/aiengr-humera/hnairesearcher. You are free to fork it, contribute to it, or use it as a reference for your own projects.
